# Loop 2.0

Loop is now a fully native Mac app: faster, lighter, and simpler. Your
projects, chats, and settings come with you.

## What's new

- Rebuilt from the ground up for macOS, with a new icon.
- Loop keeps working when you close its window, and stays in the menu bar.
- Use Loop on one Mac from another, over Tailscale.
- Claude and Codex use the sign-ins you already have on your Mac.
- New versions show up quietly at the bottom of the sidebar.

## When you update

Loop upgrades your data the first time it opens, and saves a backup first.
Older versions of Loop can't open the upgraded data. To go back within 7 days,
restore the backup in **Settings › Advanced**.

Loop 2.0 needs an Apple silicon Mac with macOS 15 or newer.
