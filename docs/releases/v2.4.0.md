# Loop v2.4.0

Agents that hand work to other agents, the Browser and Simulator from your
other Mac, a Timeline that stays smooth at any length, and a calmer window.

- **Agents delegate:** an agent can hand a task to a new chat on Claude or
  Codex, run several at once, and wait for the result or carry on and be
  woken when it's done. It can also read, message and organise your other
  chats, and schedule tasks. Delegated chats show inside the chat that started
  them, not in the sidebar; questions go to that agent, approvals always come
  to you, and Stop stops them all.
- **Agents outside Loop:** connect Claude Code or Codex in your terminal to
  Loop's tools from **Settings › Outside Agents**.
- **Usage limits:** a chat stopped by a usage limit shows when the limit
  resets and carries on by itself then, unless you cancel.
- **Browser and Simulator from your other Mac:** see them live, click, type,
  scroll, annotate and send, while the page keeps running on the Mac with
  your dev server.
- **The chat** is drawn by a new view: long chats scroll and stream smoothly,
  stay where you left them, and long replies are ordinary text you can select.
- **Work:** the Overview is now the Work tab in the side panel, with each
  Feature and ticket inside it, so the main view always shows a chat. Every
  side-panel tab can be closed, and a setting chooses the tabs a new chat
  opens with.
- **Sidebar:** project tabs are icons. Click one to show its chats, click
  again to show all.
- **Shortcuts:** ⌘N picks where a new chat goes, ⌘⇧N makes one in the current
  project, and ⌥⌘N one with no project.
- **Done:** a finished chat says Done until you open it, even while it keeps
  watching something.
- **Annotate:** one tool in the Browser: click an element or drag over an
  area, and ⌘. turns it on and off. What you picked is never lost.
- **Quote:** select words in a reply and press Quote to put them in the
  message box with your comment.
- **Pull requests:** a Pull request tab shows the chat's pull request, its
  checks and files, and merges it, deletes its branch and removes its
  worktree. Merges make a merge commit by default; choose Squash or Rebase per
  project.
- **Worktrees** get proper names, and a card in the chat shows each step while
  one is made.
- **Add Project from GitHub:** search your repositories and clone on this Mac
  or a paired one, with progress. Add an existing project to another Mac the
  same way.
- **Inbox:** a project's Inbox is the same on every Mac you've paired.
- **Workflows:** add your own instructions to a skill in an `ADDITIONS.md`
  beside it, and it still gets updates. Implementing a ticket now cleans up its
  worktree and builds. Ask Loop is gone.
- **Downloads and updates** now come from
  [loop.lytic.no](https://loop.lytic.no).

**Using Loop on two Macs?** Update both.
