# Loop v2.3.1

Fixes and polish from using 2.3 every day.

**Fixed**

- Loop no longer crashes when you pick something in the Browser.
- Chats no longer jump, go blank, or leave empty space at the bottom.
- Pictures you send while Claude is working now show up.
- Claude keeps the context of your chat, even after a restart.
- Quick Capture stays on top of the app you're using.
- Remote Macs connect even without Tailscale's command-line tool.

**Better**

- **Message box:** cleaner. The mic and Send sit inside it, and project,
  branch and model sit on one quiet line.
- **Lists:** Return sends, Shift+Return adds a bullet, Backspace removes one.
- **Dictation:** the mic button is on by default. While you speak, tap Stop
  to edit, or Send to send right away.
- **Command output** shows right in the chat, and you can copy it.
- **Work in progress** is easier to follow, with a bar that shows what's
  still running.
- **Side panel:** ⌥⌘B opens and closes it. Each chat keeps its own size, and
  a double-click on the divider makes it small.
- **Links** open in Loop's Browser. You can change this in Settings.
- **Chats** don't settle while you still have something open in them, and
  Undo brings one back.
- **Updates** tell you what they're waiting for, and let you restart now or
  later.

**New**

- Agents can add notes and screenshots to your Inbox.
