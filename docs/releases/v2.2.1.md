# Loop v2.2.1

Fixes and small improvements from using 2.2 every day.

- **Replies stay readable:** a reply no longer goes blank or leaves a large
  gap while it streams, your message never covers it, and a long chat opens
  with its messages and activity together.
- **Slow models keep working:** GPT 6.1 sol and other slow models no longer
  time out. While one is thinking, the chat says "Waiting for Codex · GPT
  6.1 sol".
- **Copy and paste:** copying a reply pastes into Slack, Notes, and Mail as
  formatted text with its lists, bold, and links. Pasted lists keep their
  lines and nesting.
- **Settled:** chats are sorted and aged by when you last used them, not when
  you settled them. A chat that's been quiet for 30 days settles itself;
  choose 7, 14, 30, or 90 days, or Off, in Settings. A chat you unsettle
  stays put.
- **Background tasks:** stop one from the chat with the ✕ next to it, and new
  icons for background work and subagents.
- **Files:** open ready to edit, show a dot when unsaved, ask Save, Discard,
  or Cancel before you leave, and show their folder as breadcrumbs.
- **Window:** panels open smaller and reset with a double-click on their
  divider. New thread is back beside Search, the title bar stays in place,
  and images can be dropped anywhere in a chat, the composer included.
- **Updates:** Loop comes to the front after it updates itself, and shows one
  message in the middle of the window while it upgrades your data.
- **⌘K** filters the sidebar to the project you open, and puts your filter
  back after you send.
- **Project logos** for Mac apps.
